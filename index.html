Barrier Protection & Application Tip

Safe Application & When to Seek Help

  • Introduce only 1 active skincare product at a time (2–3 nights per week).
  • Always patch test new products on your inner arm or neck for 24 hours before facial use.
  • Discontinue use immediately if stinging, persistent redness, burning, or peeling occurs.
  • If skin symptoms persist or worsen after 4 to 6 weeks, consult a board-certified dermatologist for a clinical assessment.
Medical Notice: This guidance is provided for general cosmetic skincare reference only. It is not a medical diagnosis, prescription, or clinical treatment plan. For persistent, severe, or concerning medical skin conditions, consult a board-certified dermatologist.
